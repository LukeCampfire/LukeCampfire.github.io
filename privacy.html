Privacy policy

4WD 24-7 Master. Last updated 28 September 2026.

What the app does

4WD 24-7 Master is an internal tool run by Campfire Creative for the 4WD 24/7 YouTube channel. When the channel owner connects it, it reads the channel's YouTube data and YouTube Analytics reports, including views, watch time, subscribers, revenue, impressions and click-through rate, and writes them into a private Google Sheet used by the 4WD 24/7 team.

What data we access

Read-only access to the 4WD 24/7 channel's YouTube data and YouTube Analytics reports. The app cannot upload, edit or delete videos, and it does not access Gmail, Google Drive files outside its own sheet, contacts or any other personal data.

How the data is used

The data is used only to report on the 4WD 24/7 channel's performance for the team's own planning. It is stored in a private Google Sheet that only the 4WD 24/7 team can open.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone outside the 4WD 24/7 team, and we do not use it for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Data already in the Google Sheet can be deleted by deleting the sheet.
